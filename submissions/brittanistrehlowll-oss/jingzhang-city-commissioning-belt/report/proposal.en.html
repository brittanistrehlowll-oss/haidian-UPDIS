JING-ZHANG CITY COMMISSIONING BELT

A city has no single standard answer. Human and silicon intelligence share an aspiration for better life.

Urban planning and real-world scenario innovation for the AI era. Urban design and daily life are the subject; the Civic AI Overlay is the primary planning innovation; Compute–Power–Water is professional infrastructure support; C0–C6 and the City Evidence Ledger are operating, verification and traceability methods.

The current spatial model uses the organizer-maintained provisional_rough boundary and is not an official redline. Areas, ratios, massing and parcel-level conclusions must be recalculated once official polygons, statutory controls, ownership, heritage and engineering information become available.

1. Overall Structure

Layer 1 | Urban-design subject: the Jing-Zhang heritage-park public spine + Zhongzhiyuan / AI Origin / Dazhongsi + the official Three Zones and Two Wings. Public space, daily life, industry and incremental renewal must work first; AI is overlaid on that real city.

Layer 2 | Civic AI Overlay: an additional regulatory-plan layer for AI-era planning objects: edge-compute interfaces, energy-flexibility interfaces, robot/logistics curb interfaces, sensing boundaries, operating permissions, human override, low/no-sensing routes, versioned evidence, re-test and exit conditions. The minimum rule unit is Rule ID → Spatial Scope → Object → Requirement/KPI → Source → Responsible Party → Permission → Gate → Evidence → Version → Re-test → Exit.

Layer 3 | AI Infrastructure Support: only C | Compute Matching, P | Compute–Power Coordination and W | Compute–Water Coordination are elevated. Communications, underground utilities, transport and logistics remain engineering and scenario-enabling conditions rather than a restored “six-network” master narrative.

Layer 4 | C0–C6 + Evidence Ledger: move already-planned AI capabilities from evidence baseline and simulation through rule checks and shadow operation to limited real load, human-supervised operation, recertification, rollback and exit. They are operating methods, not the headline originality claim.

2. Official Task Alignment

Three positionings: Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt / Metropolitan AI Life Experience Belt / AI Fusion Innovation Belt.

Five functions: full-stack AI indigenous innovation / world-class AI innovation ecosystem / new AI+ scenario paradigm / intelligent and vibrant AI city / global discourse capacity in AI governance.

Three Zones and Two Wings: Zhongzhiyuan validates technology; AI Origin validates real life; Dazhongsi validates renewal and rules; the Zhongguancun technology-service wing links research, IP, capital and professional services; the Xiaoyue River scenario-enablement wing links ecology, communities, municipal systems and real urban use.

4. AI Infrastructure Adaptation

C | Compute Matching: regional compute for large-scale training and high-intensity tasks; district-level services for inference, digital twins and shared R&D; edge nodes for low-latency, privacy-sensitive and high-reliability tasks. The central city does not default to new large AIDC facilities.

P | Compute–Power Coordination: Protected / Flexible / Migratable compute loads are coordinated with power conditions, storage, building load flexibility and task migration.

W | Compute–Water Coordination: W0–W3 spatial adaptation rules address cooling mode, water-source conditions, heat rejection and review triggers without inventing MW, MVA, m³/d, pipe diameters or engineering alignments.

5. Public Interest and Civic Nodes

Basic public service aims for outcome equivalence across AI, human, offline and low/no-sensing routes. The three core honor nodes are First Run Yard, Open Evidence Hall and Civic Override Beacon; Commissioning Commons is a supporting shared venue.

6. Scenarios, Personas and Operations

The proposal contains 12 formal AI+ scenario cards, including four industrial/planning test-validation scenarios; eight persona categories; and seven global mechanism comparisons. Long-term operation includes Commissioning Week, Open Urban AI Plugfest, Civic Audit Open Day and Rulebook Release.

7. Spatial Model and Implementation

The design model includes site_boundary, key_areas, land_use, buildings, roads, green_space, public_space, constraints and phasing, plus a landmarks point layer. Current provisional model metrics are approximately 11.41 km² site area, 17.6% green ratio and 6.7% public-space ratio. They are not approved statutory indicators.

Implementation uses NOW / NEXT / CONDITION-BASED packages to distinguish reversible actions possible now, next-stage pilots, and projects dependent on formal planning, engineering, capacity data and approval.

8. Evidence and Limits

The full bilingual proposal is in proposal.md / proposal.en.md. Machine-readable evidence is carried by compliance_matrix.json, standard_matrix.json, design_depth_matrix.json, metrics.json, sources.json, assumptions.json and GeoJSON. Where FAR, height, ownership, heritage controls, municipal capacity or official road data are unavailable, values remain unknown or explicitly limited rather than being fabricated by AI.

This is an open co-creation urban-design proposal for subsequent professional deepening. It is not an approval, statutory-plan conclusion, engineering feasibility study or implementation commitment.
