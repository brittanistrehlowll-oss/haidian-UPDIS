JING-ZHANG CITY COMMISSIONING BELT

A city has no single standard answer. Human and silicon intelligence share an aspiration for better life.

The proposal combines a Civic AI Overlay, seven C0-C6 commissioning gates and a City Evidence Ledger so that urban AI remains testable, auditable, human-overridable, reversible and exitable.

The current geometry uses the organizer-maintained provisional_rough boundary and is not an official redline. Areas, ratios and parcel-level conclusions must be recalculated when official polygons, regulatory controls, ownership, heritage and engineering data become available.

1. Overall Structure

One spine, two planes, three urban labs, seven gates and one evidence ledger. The Jing-Zhang heritage park becomes a continuous public spine. The Physical City Plane carries water, energy, compute, communications, utilities and mobility; the Institutional AI Plane carries evidence, simulation, design, operation and deliberation. The Civic AI Overlay links the two.

C0 Evidence Baseline → C1 Simulation → C2 Rule Check → C3 Shadow/Dummy Load → C4 Limited Real Load → C5 Human-supervised Operation → C6 Recertification/Rollback/Exit.

2. Three Scope Levels

  • 43.6 km² strategic study area: industry, research, talent, infrastructure and real urban scenarios.
  • Approx. 11.41 km² overall design area: public spine, east-west stitching, AI infrastructure interfaces and incremental renewal.
  • Three key areas: Zhongzhiyuan CITY TESTBED, Beijing AI Origin Community LIVING LOAD, and Dazhongsi RENEWAL RULE LAB.

4. Three Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan: a pre-deployment urban testbed for AI infrastructure interoperability, robotics/autonomous logistics and municipal-AI shadow operation.

AI Origin Community: real daily loads including housing, education, health, accessibility, commuting and public services, with phone, in-person and human fallback routes preserved.

Dazhongsi: a renewal-rule lab with planning deliberation, district-parcel optimization, machine-readable rule checking and evidence-led renewal; AI simulation does not replace real public participation.

5. Spatial Model and Metrics

The submission contains nine design layers: site_boundary, key_areas, land_use, buildings, roads, green_space, public_space, constraints and phasing. Core recomputable provisional design-model metrics are approximately 11.41 km² site area, 17.6% green ratio and 6.7% public-space ratio. They are not approved statutory indicators.

6. Risk and Governance

Critical AI services use NORMAL / DEGRADED / MANUAL / EXIT states. Sensing capability does not equal control authority, and technical feasibility does not equal institutional authorization. High-risk actions affecting mobility, utilities, energy and public services require human approval, auditability and rollback.

Full bilingual narratives are in proposal.md and proposal.en.md. Machine-readable evidence is carried by compliance_matrix.json, standard_matrix.json, design_depth_matrix.json, metrics.json, sources.json, assumptions.json and the nine GeoJSON layers.
